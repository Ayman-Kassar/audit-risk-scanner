TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY - Effective January 2026

1. FLIGHT BOOKINGS
All flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance through the approved travel portal.
Business class is permitted only for flights exceeding 6 hours and requires CFO approval.
Economy class is the default for all domestic and short-haul European flights.

2. HOTEL ACCOMMODATION
Maximum hotel rate: Stockholm 2,500 SEK per night, London $250 per night, New York $300 per night.
Hotels must be booked through the approved portal to qualify for reimbursement.
Airbnb and private accommodation are not reimbursable.

3. MEALS AND ENTERTAINMENT
Daily meal allowance: 500 SEK for domestic travel, €75 for international travel.
Client entertainment requires pre-approval from department head for amounts over 2,000 SEK.
Alcohol is reimbursable only during approved client entertainment - not during internal meetings.

4. CAR AND TRANSPORT
Rental cars require manager approval for rentals exceeding 3 days.
Mileage reimbursement rate: 25 SEK per kilometer for personal car use.
Taxis and Uber are reimbursable for business travel - not for commuting to regular office.

5. REIMBURSEMENT PROCESS
All expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
Receipts are required for all expenses over 200 SEK.
Expenses submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed without CFO exception approval.

6. CREDIT CARDS
Company credit cards are issued to employees at manager level and above.
Personal purchases on company credit cards must be repaid within 5 business days.
Lost or stolen cards must be reported to finance within 2 hours of discovery.

7. AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE
All expense reports are subject to random audit.
False or inflated expense claims will result in immediate disciplinary action.
Repeat policy violations will result in removal of expense privileges.
